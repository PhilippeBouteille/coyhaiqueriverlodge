Welcome

Where passion meets Patagonian nature.

Set on 40 acres (16 hectares), 5 miles (8 km) from Coyhaique and on the banks of the Coyhaique River, your trip unfolds against a backdrop of natural splendor: a refuge for anglers and nature lovers alike.

Our story

More than two decades building a Patagonian tradition.

Coyhaique River Lodge was born from a simple conviction: Patagonia is best experienced up close, unhurried, and with people who have known its rivers, forests and seasons for generations. Two decades later, that same conviction still guides every season, every itinerary and every guest who arrives looking for more than a vacation.
